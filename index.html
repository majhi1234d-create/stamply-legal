Privacy policy

Provider and contact

Stamply is provided by Digital Company, s.r.o., company ID 05573157, Pohoří 137, 518 01 Pohoří, Czech Republic. Support and privacy contact: digitalcompanysro@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android app and is dated 4 November 2026.

Photos and local content

The camera processes the live preview and captured photos. Originals, edited photos, notes, projects, imported logos, styles and PDFs are stored on the phone. The app does not automatically upload them to the provider and has no own cloud or registration. Optionally, time, address and coordinates are written into the photo and local record. Shared photos and PDFs may contain this information.

Optional location, address and map

Location is used only when location features are enabled and permission is granted, while the app is in use; we do not track location in the background. Approximate or precise location can be allowed. For addresses we send coordinates to the Android Geocoder system service; its provider depends on the device and may process coordinates and technical data under its own policy. The map requests tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org. OpenStreetMap Foundation and its delivery network receive the IP address, requested map area, time and app identification. The requested area can reveal approximate location. Revoke permission in Android settings; address and map features can also be disabled separately.

Purchases and review access

Google Play handles the one-time Photo PLUS purchase and payment details. Stamply does not receive the card number. Licence verification and restoration use the product, status, signed purchase record and purchase token; the record is stored locally and purchase acknowledgement is sent to Google Play. Google Play libraries may process technical app, device, connection and purchase-flow information, including diagnostics. The review code is verified and stored locally only and is not sent to the provider.

Sharing, support and security

Files are passed to another app only when you choose to share. A service configured on your phone may independently back up gallery copies. Diagnostics are not sent automatically: a button copies them to the clipboard and you can send them to support yourself. Email support processes the sender address, message and attachments through Gmail. Do not send passwords or card details. The app has no advertising SDK or own usage analytics. Android permissions protect local files; automatic backup of private app data is disabled. Direct map requests use HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

Private content, settings and licences remain until removed, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; purchase records also change with licence verification. Individual photos can be deleted in the app. Clearing app data removes PDFs, imported logos and other local remnants. Map cache is reused for up to one week; files may remain until the cache is cleared. Gallery copies, exports and already shared files must be deleted separately; uninstalling does not remove them. Clearing data is irreversible: export important photos first. Support correspondence is retained while handling the request and thereafter only as necessary for legal claims or duties. Google and other providers set their own retention periods.

Purposes, legal bases and your rights

We process data needed to provide the app, support and licences to perform the contract; legal-claim protection and security rely on legitimate interests, and mandatory records on legal obligations. You initiate optional features; any consent can be withdrawn and permissions revoked. Denying location does not prevent basic photography. We do not perform profiling or automated decisions with legal effects. Under applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, or object. Contact the email above; we cannot remotely access files stored only on your phone. You can complain to the Czech data protection authority (uoou.gov.cz) or your local supervisory authority.

External providers and changes

Google, the system location provider and OpenStreetMap Foundation operate under their own policies. Their services and delivery networks may process data outside the EEA; their policies linked below describe transfers and safeguards. The provider does not sell photo content or supply it for advertising. We update this policy and its date when features change; significant changes will be announced in the app.
